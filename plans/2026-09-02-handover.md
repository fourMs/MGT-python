# Handover — 2026-09-02

1.30.0 is published on PyPI: the postures layer (`segment_postures`, `key_postures`,
`average_posture`, `match_postures`), detector-agnostic `normalise_poses`, and the
jitter-robust stationarity measure that the dance corpus forced. The two things that
matter beyond the feature list are documents, and both have consequences outside this
repository.

**The terminology charter is now the law of the prose.**
`plans/2026-09-01-terminology-charter.md` holds ARJ's sharp terms — the four levels,
the two ladders (motion/action/gesture over position/posture/pose), the
percept–physics pairs, the rhythm cluster, coupling against mapping against
correlation — plus the addendum recording a seven-corpus sweep (~190 fixes) and his
six rulings, all applied. The rulings a maintainer here must know: `Action` spans
stay, as estimates that need interpretation; `body_audio_coupling()` and
`warp_audiovisual_beats()` are retired names that warn and delegate to
`motion_audio_coupling()` and `warp_audiomotion_beats()` until 2.0; the measured
quantity is motion, RMS is a level, and a file has no timbre. The shareable rulings
record is at https://claude.ai/code/artifact/9c403f5a-6e0d-4f53-8ece-f3008cc58895.

**The documentation is reorganised, and the division of labour is explicit.** The
wiki teaches (numbered chapters in pedagogical order, tutorial prose migrated in);
the site answers (task references, the Concepts chapter, the Gallery as front door,
the API menu grouped by the ladder). Seven User Guide pages shrank by 1,531 lines
into task references; every inbound anchor was kept and verified. Two pages never
had wiki chapters to absorb them — `core-classes.md` and `audio-video.md` — and
whether they get chapters is an open ARJ question in the dance project's TODO.

**Pending for the next release** (changelog Unreleased is already written): the
audio–motion renames with their deprecation wrappers, the terminology sweep of
docstrings and figure labels, and the `subject`→`participant` prose. Nothing
unreleased changes behaviour beyond wording and warnings.

**Sequencing** stays with `plans/2026-08-30-development-plan.md`: 2.0 cuts June
2027, removals only (the deprecation wrappers and retired attribute aliases go
then), paired with the JOSS submission. ReID v2 shipped in 1.29.0; v2.1
(appearance-bridged mover-chains across breaks) is designed in spirit but unpriced.

**One trap renewed this week, in two projects at once**: a generator pointed at
files someone hand-edited. The soundscape project's `readmes.py` had silently
fallen 161 lines behind its READMEs and deleted them when run; the same class of
fault was caught here in August. Before running any generator, ask whose file it
is; before trusting one, diff its output against what is on disk.
